Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** The party was hired by the Vermillion Trading Company to remove pirates from the village of Seadust on the Menagerie Coast. After travelling to the village, the party encountered a large group of pirates and witnessed an exchange between their "captain" and a mysterious undead. A combat ensued wherein the pirate's wizard and captain were slain, while the undead and the rest of the pirates escaped. The party was paid handsomely for their efforts. 
**Important World Impacts:** A mysterious undead retrieved a piece of wood from the village.
