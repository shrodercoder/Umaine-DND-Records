Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** The party tracked the child of a farmer kidnapped by a "Ferryman". The party tracked them into the Dwendalian Empire to Trostenwald where they confronted this mysterious figure. The figure offered Glee Voynich and Reza Dreamgrove seats at Soltryce Academy seeming to produce all the proper paperwork to have the authority to make such a decision. The party split ways in Zadash with the wizards heading to Rexxentrum and the rest of the party heading back to Port Damali. 
A contract was signed between Glee and the Ferryman. Contract Terms: 
- Both Glee Voynich and Reza are permitted to attend Soltryce Academy 
- Both swear no fealty to the empire but must remain in good standing with the law 
- Glee is allowed to visit the child regularly while at the academy. 
- Terrance Silovar will be supplied housing and transportation to visit his child Marthus at Soltryce Academy 
**Important World Impacts:** Glee Voynich and Reza Dreamgrove are now students of the Soltryce Academy after being recruited by a Ferryman. There is also a child of extreme magical power being inducted into the academy.
