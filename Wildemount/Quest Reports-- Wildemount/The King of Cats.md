Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** A member of the Port Damali Congress of Cats, Asparagus, hired the party to find their lost King, Meowtholemew the Great. The Congress of Cats had a tenuous alliance with the Worg Lords, a pack of stray dogs led by the scrappy Chihuahua Paco, which would fail if Meowtholemew did not return to the Congress by sunrise. After tracking the King through the Clockwork Carnival sideshow at the Gilded Esplanade, they found where the king was being held prisoner and rescued him from both The Rat Boss, a wererat halfling who leads the vermin factions of creatures, and from Asparagus himself, who was trying to ruin the peace and ursurp the king. The Rat Boss Escaped, but Asparagus was killed in the conflict.  
**Important World Impacts:** Within Port Damali, stray cats and dogs have an uneasy peace. Both factions have declared war on the vermin, vowing to kill any rats they see on sight. The Rat Boss is still at large....
