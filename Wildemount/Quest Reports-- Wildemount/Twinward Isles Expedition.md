Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** The party responded to a call for help from a Ki'Nau representative named Pelenato to track down where protected resources are being harvested from at Twinward Isles and who/what group is doing so. Pelenato explained that this will be a multi-week effort as the Twinward Isles are far too large to explore in a single week and he wants the party to focus on finding out where the harvesting took place to aid future expeditions. The party explored two possible sites and dealt with any active threats at each site. The party reported their findings to Pelenato and came to the conclusion that something weird is happening on the island and they need to investigate further. The party chose the northern tip of Twinward Isles as a base camp for future expeditions. 
**Important World Impacts:**
- A small temporary Ki'Nau camp will be constructed on the northern tip of the Twinward isles.
- The lumber and mineral economy of [[Port Damali]] took a large hit from the exploitation of protected resources being introduced to the market. The prices will return to their normal states in a matter of days.
