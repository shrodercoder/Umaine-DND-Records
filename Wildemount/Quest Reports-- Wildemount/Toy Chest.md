Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** The party was hired by Monty Curtel, a dwarven Clovis Concord worker, to bring a magical toy into the woods and attempt to enter the magical Circus that has popped up. The party was invited in by the clowns, played games, and preformed a service for the organizer Mr. Bingles in exchange for getting some answers. The party was attacked by a slime tendril in a ballpit and feral clowns, a tough battle, and then conversed with the head of security Shawn Sledge. 
**Important World Impacts:** Various things about the circus have been learned, including that people from across the oceans are visiting.
