**Race:** Astral Elf
**Class:** Astral Self Monk 8 Fighter 1

**Backstory:** Joel Van Morrison was raised from birth as a follower of Bahamut, high in the Sunderpeak Monastery in Issylra. Believing wholeheartedly in the story the elders told him, Joel was convinced he was delivered to the steps of the temple by stork, with the name "Joel" written hastily on a tag. As Joel's childhood progressed, he would begin to participate in the same practices as the rest of the conclave: meditation, prayer to the Platinum Dragon, and intense physical and mental training meant to tap into latent potential inside creatures colloquially referred to as "Ki". Isolated from the rest of the continent and neighboring villages, the monks of the Sunderpeak live peaceful lives in close-knit, trusting groups. Because of this upbringing, Joel tends to see the best in everyone even when it's not there. As Joel aged into maturity, he eventually adopted the surname "Van Morrison" after one of the monastery's founding members. At the tail end of Joel's training, he would set out for pilgrimage to the wider world to lend aid to any who would need it and hone his skills. This pilgrimage would take him to Port Damali in Wildemount, where he would join the Coinbound in an attempt to broaden his understanding of the world outside of the Sunderpeaks and lend aid to any who’d need it.

**Coinbound Work:** Joel would begin his work in the Coinbound and quickly find that his peaceful and insightful brethren of the monastery were, in fact, not the norm, and that the world outside of the Sunderpeaks is harsh, violent, and greedy. He would learn this firsthand in his missions starting in the Coinbound, and oftentimes, the Coinbound themselves would be the worst of the offenders. As the months progressed, Joel would grow in strength, with Joel’s Astral Form honing its shape into a visage matching that of a Metallic Dragon. Eventually, Joel would undergo a mission where he would encounter an organization known as The Scarlet Wing alongside Coinbound members Arvad and Gaxin Shovar, who Joel would form a bond with as he continued to combat the Scarlet Wing…

**To Have-Not:** The conflict with the Have-Not would come to a head when Joel would join what appeared to be a simple quest to retrieve wine from Feolinn for a tavern, which quickly spiralled into an intense conflict with the Scarlet Wing. Joined by Coinbound Members Arvad, Gaxin, Reza, Kael, and Ransom, the group would travel to Feolinn and find the town in the midst of crisis with food supplies running short and trade routes crawling with nefarious bandits. It is during their first night in Feolinn that Joel was attacked in the street late at night by a mysterious man known as Oskha, who would later be discovered as The Have-Not himself. Joel would survive the encounter, though scared, and still naively wished to forgive and redeem his assailant after encountering him in the town square feeding the needy. Joel believed that this man was simply misguided and hoped that Oskha’s best interests lay with the people of the struggling town after finding him providing food to the needy. Hoping to solve the famine, the party would seek aid from the town's elite, eventually finding themselves in the good graces of the Marquis of Feolinn. However, it was during these negotiations that the Scarlet Wing would make its move, besieging the town and attempting to kill the Marquis. We would save the Marquis and escort him to the safety of the guardhouse. Rallying what forces we could, the party would stand against The Have-Not and eventually defeat him and his forces; the party would be celebrated as heroes of Feolinn and return to Port Damali.

**Time Betrays:** Joel would continue to oppose the Scarlet Wing when he and Gaxin were called back to Feolinn, joined by Coinbound members, Sir Reggie, Crystal, Bubbadawabadub, and Violet. The group would venture into the peaks of the Cyrios Mountains to flush out Scarlet Wing that had dug into the old ruins. After scouting the hideout, the party, joined by a small force sent by Feolinn, would pierce the defenses of the hideout and uncover the Wing’s plans. Doing battle against a myriad of the Wing’s officers, the party would retreat with the knowledge gained and report back to the Marquis.

**Fire and Ice:** After trial and tribulation, Joel would be invited back to the Sunderpeak Monastery by his mentors Alessi and Steel, after word of Joel’s endeavors reached back to the monks. Joined alongside his friends Crystal, Bubba, and Reggie, they would journey to Joel’s home and undergo 3 trials in order to claim the Wraps of the Dyamak. Throughout these trials, Joel’s Astral Form would evolve to greater heights, and with the Wraps accepting him, Joel and friends would undergo one final labor, defeating a draconic cult that had taken form in the time of Joel’s absence. Venturing to the volcanic lair of the nefarious drake, the party would face off against the chromatic dragon, Geryon, besting him in human form, dragon form, and skeletal form. Finally slaying the resilient beast, the party would celebrate, returning back to Port Damali, with a weight of responsibility now resting on Joel’s shoulders as he carries the heirloom of his home, hoping to continue to do good and help the people of Wildmount.
