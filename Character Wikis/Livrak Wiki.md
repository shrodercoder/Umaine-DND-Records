# Livrak Deepbreaker Ghost of the Leviathan
Character Wiki: 
Livrak comes from a sea fairing Goliath race who are decedents of storm giants who lived by the coast. When Livrak was commanding his pirate galleon the triten through a storm he fell into the deep dark waters and found something glowing calling him. once he touched it parts of his body started turning blue and his body started growing new body parts. Ever since this incident he’s dawned armor that slowly chips away so that no one can see his appearance For Livrak his family is his father who is a large Goliath of 10ft tall and his mother was 7f tall and his brothers are 8ft like him. Livrak has always had great respect for his family since they were always a unit his 2 brothers joined their family business of fishing and monster hunting for food and money where he hounded his skills on the sea. His father Cetitan Deepbreaker and his mother Aurora Deepbreaker were disappointed when they heard of Livraks escapades as a pirate captain but they later came around and turned a blind eye to it since Livrak would come back frequently with fish, beasts, and treasure from the deep sea. His brothers Xiphias and Rhincodon Deepbreaker wanted to join Livrak but He adamantly refused due to him not wanting his brothers to be in danger since he lost men every few voyages. After the sinking of the Triten and his disfiguration he came home afraid to show his face but his family even though very surprised welcomed him back and helped him to adjust to his new powers and strength. His main source of strength is his son Carchadonus.
