Dodaro started his life with no family. He was born to a very rich set of parents; he was their only child. He grew up with his brother and sister until their entire family met a tragic end when he was old. They were a part of a secret rebellion attempting to save a lost princess who he was set to marry. He never wound up marrying her or having any children really. His two brothers wound up having children though. Unfortunately his brothers died when they were four because of the plague that killed the rest of his family. Ever since Dodaro was born he grew on the streets completely alone living an un interesting life until the coin bound.

Once Dodaro reached adulthood his life was filled with adventure. Once during his sleep he was taken by aliens. They took him underground for experimentation with multiple other people. Upon him and the others escaping their goo tubes he was the only one who remembered how they got there he explained how they were on a flying saucer zooming into outer space. They all later successfully escaped. Next came his time as leader of the mole people. The Lizard people were in disarray their leader was recently poisoned and a great battle approaching Dodaro stepped up to lead. He joined by marrying the king of dolls leading them all to victory. He also became Joseph Joestar

As Dodaro traveled he met various people and had many children. In order to protect their mothers though he used aliases with them which they later renamed their children after. There is Aotaro the second, botaro the second, moterbotaro (who is half rat) the second, cotaro the second, eotaro the second, fotaro the second, gotaro the second, hotaro the second, iotaro the second, jotaro the second, kotaro the second (twin to cotaro the second), lotaro the second, motaro the second, notaro the second, ootaro the second, potaro the second, qotaro the second, rotaro the second, sotaro the second, throataro the second (who cannot speak), uotaro the second, votaro the second, wotaro the second, xotaro the second, yotaro the second, zotaro the second. One of whom is currently in Bubbas Orphanage. 

Later in life Dodaro found the coinbound where he has gone on many quests and met interesting people. One of the most memorable being his pet Blast. He met Blast on multiple occasions all of which Blast left him to die in completely avoidable circumstances. Blast later attempted to shoot Dodaro and missed despite Dodaro not having much armor. Blast attempted to play it cool acting like it was a warning shot but Dodaro knows the real reason he missed is because Blast is scared of the dark.

Later on Dodaro met Jorge and the two did drugs together. During their drug trip Jorge invited Dodaro to this new thing called The Seven Wonders. Dodaro joined the seven wonders and they all went on adventure together. During their adventure Dodaro stabbed himself in the chest twice with a shiny knife leaving gaping holes in his chest. Later when that knife became a woman Dodaro immediately became disinterested in having her inside him. Later attempting to wing man his good friend Siegfried to her so that Siegfried could remove the armor he really wanted that she had on. While they were in Draconia for this adventure Dodaro summoned two pyramids and a beanstalk (accidently knocking over the beanstalk across the ruins of Draconia) and he discovered slaves could own property. This quest eventually came to an end though with a portal to hell. While doing his best to close the portal though Dodaro happened to accidentally toss a bean through summoning a statue of himself in hell which told the tales of all the great deeds he had done to the demons. After closing the portal the seven wonders returned home to where Dodaro's good friend Shade married him to his 13 spouses and where he has been resting awaiting to go on his next great adventure.
