**Race: **Human 
**Class:** Glory Paladin 9

**Backstory:**
Hailing from the northern shipping town of Icehaven, Jorge Ramalo served as the bard for a hometown hero, who carried the moniker "Bobby Fabulous, Hairdo King, Fashion Tsar, Wonder of the World". Despite his mentor failings to amass a large of a reputation as his name would suggest, Jorge always aspired to take after his teacher. Eventually, Bobby Fabulous would reach an age where his adventuring days would be behind him, and Jorge would jovially take up his old friend's namesake, and vow to make the name Bobby Fabulous one known and remembered far and wide. Using what teachings he took from his stead as a sidekick, Jorge completed odd jobs and quests as he journeyed across the Dwendalian Empire, making his way to Port Damali, and joining the Coinbound. 

**Coinbound Work:**
During his work as a Coinbound, Jorge has managed to amass quite a reputation, during one of his earliest quests he was sent to New Haxon where he would meet Shade, Sazantos, Billard, and Dodaro, persons that would work with him to calm the tempers of the unsettled townsfolk from reaching a boiling point after a morally grey artificer wished to test weapons on animals. Jorge believed that these Coinbound, like him, had the makings of glorious heroes and so would be formed what were the founding members of The 7 Wonders. Later on, Jorge and Dodaro would work to stop a nefarious botanist from enslaving a town using magical spores from a plant monster. 

Soon after these events two more members would join the 7 Wonders, Siegfried and Challie after they worked with Sazantos on a mission. 

With the Wonders assembled they would portion off some of their earnings from the Coinbound to construct their headquarters, The Hall of Wonder, which lies in the northern section of the Gilded Esplanade in Port Damali.

**Wonders Of Our Time:**

Now an established group, The 7 Wonders would take on their first true quest. Hired by a former watchman of Draconia, they were tasked with escorting survivors from the city back to the remains of their home. Sailing East, The Wonders would encounter a stowaway, a small Draconblood by the name of Victor Za Dragwell, who the Wonders would befriend as they arrive in the city. Marching to the city, The Wonders would fend off hoards of Demons that were entering the city through a portal and mighty Chromatic Dragons. The Wonders would defeat these threats as swiftly as they would arrive with their combined powers. Eventually, The Wonders would work with Victor and survivors of Draconia, Varkuun, Morrax, and Vorrack to finally close the portal for good. Collecting relics from Draconia, The Wonders would battle with The Former Baroness of Draconia, who was working towards a mysterious and nefarious goal. It was in this battle that Jorge Ramalo would gain possession of The Titanstone Knuckles, after receiving a vision of the past users protecting the innocent. With the proper tools in their hand, The Wonders would siege the portal with the aim of closing it for good. During this long fought battle, The Baroness would attempt to open the portal wider, unleashing a powerful demonic dragon that would swallow Sazantos and Morrax. Jorge, without second thought, would leap into the jaws of this beast to reclaim his friends and, with the help of Dodaro, Jorge would grab Morrax and his hammer, and gloriously bust through the jaw of the beast with a mighty swing, saving his friends from digestion. The portal would be closed and the Wonders would escort the survivors and return to Port Damali as Heroes of Draconia

**Collateral Annihilation:**

Returning to Port Damali, Jorge would take upon a quest against the nefarious organization known as the Scarlet Wing and the reputable pirate group known as the Revelry. A team of Coinbound were assembled which included most notable Gaxin Shovar, Zada, and a rookie adventurer named Myrmex. The party was sent to the Vide Cay to investigate the two organizations, on the way running into the Revalry on the way. The pirates however grossly underestimated our vessel, as Feaco sank their first vessel with a casting of control water, and Jorge sank the second with a cannon ball enhanced with the power of the Titanstone Knuckles. We arrived at the Vide Cay to be briefed on the situation, we sailed on to investigate the last known location of the Revelry who we found held up in a cave after an encounter with the Wing left them battered and their numbers reduced. We eliminated their hideout with a small cameo from two Wing Officer’s, who decided not to engage. Leaving to investigate the Wing’s last known location, we stumbled into a third Wing Officer, who claimed that they were planning on taking the whole island by force. Our quest came to a head as we defended the village from the invading Scarlet Wing, who through the power of our glorious resolve were bested and forced to retreat. The battle left most of us low on stamina, as Wing Commander, Mahrez, appeared and did battle with us, even with our exhausted party, we were able to gain the upper hand as Mahrez stood upon his last legs. Before we could fully claim victory against the Scarlet Wing., two more officers appeared as Jore’s friends began to retreat, viewing the battle as lost. Jorge remained until the last of the party was able to safely leave, as he stood as the last warrior against the approaching Scarlet forces, he smiled, raised his sword, and taunted his foes, knowing that this would be his last wonderful stand…
