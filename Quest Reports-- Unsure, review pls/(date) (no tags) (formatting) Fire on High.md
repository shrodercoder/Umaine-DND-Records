Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** Teddy Fazvelt (and friends) were hired by Maria Foreman, the head of a noble family in Felderwin, whose property came under attack by an arsonist. After resting in a roadside tavern outside the town and stopping a group of incompetent bandits known as The Motley Crue, the party questioned the noble families of The Tillage for leads. This led them to a goblin camp tucked away in the east. While they didn't find any evidence linking the goblins to the fire, they did, however, find a kidnapped noble, which gave them favor with another wealthy family. During a celebratory dinner the next day, the party was given a lead. Beneath the earth of Felderwin lie tunnels from [[The War of Ash and Light]], which the party scoured for clues, after a deadly fight with the underground inhabitants, they discovered a hideaway far in the tunnels. Unearthing evidence that the criminal is a doppelganger posing as one of the townsfolk. With this newfound information, the party makes haste to subdue the threat, only to find that another fire has started. After quelling the flames, the party, hot on the doppelgangers' heels, makes haste to track down the culprit as they look to flee the Dwendalian Empire through the Ashkeeper Peaks. It all comes to a close when the two parties face off in a cave system underneath the mountains, where they are able to finally subdue the threat and bring his body back to Felderwin to confirm his death with The Foremans.
**Important World Impacts** The fires started by the Doppelganger have damaged several acres of farming land in Felderwin. Additionally, the party unearthed evidence that the Doppelganger was in contact with The [[Kryn Dynasty]] during his infiltration.
