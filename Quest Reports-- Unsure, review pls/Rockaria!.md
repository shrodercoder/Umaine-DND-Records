Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** A group of Coinbound was hired by the owners of the Rockaria Opera House to fill in for a slew of actors and bodyguards who quit. The party put on quite the roaring show before it was interrupted by a mysterious individual and a horde of Specters. The adventurers were able to fend off the undead, but not before two audience members perished in the chaos. The perpetrator also escaped before they could be questioned about their motives. 
**Important World Impacts:** In light of the deaths that occurred at The Rockaria Opera House. The building now has a reputation for being haunted, which has caused the business to lose money and further damage its already faltering public image.
