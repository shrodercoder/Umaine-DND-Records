Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** The party was hired to be security detail for a local history museum under the threat of an imminent break-in. They discovered a number of creatures that animated at night, as well as a Galeb Duhr guarding a large ruby. They fended off the intruders, killing many, and afterwards reported the museum owner, Mr. Max, to the authorities. Mr. Max was arrested for illegal possessions of magical goods. 
**Important World Impacts:** A strange woman with glowing eyes stole a piece of a ship's wheel from the museum. In addition, the town guard is sending regular interns into the museum's basement storage to appropriate the remaining magic items.
