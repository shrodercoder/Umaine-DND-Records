Brief Overview: "The party was hired by dwarven Clovis Conclave offical Monty Curtel to escort a negotiator from Feolinn back to Port Damali. They were given partial payment upfront to buy supplies and given a carriage plus expert driver, and over the passage of four days they gambled, talked, problem solved and stumbled upon a silver box filled with golden tickets to Mr. Bingles Festival. The party eventually got to Feolinn and decided to take a rest before heading back, only to learn the tickets magically distributed themselves amongst the party and escorts. An assembly of clowns, costumed characters and horse mounted knights ambushed the party and swiftly knocked them out. They party and the representatives guards woke up inside a train themed hotel with breakfast items inside the bedside drawers. They learned from the hotel staff that they are special guests invited to play games and take part in an activity involving Mr. Bingles, the festival owner. Over the span of three days, the group traversed through the constant partying and non-stop fun, earning tickets through games and sudden fights, searching for funnel cake, and chasing after Mr. Bingles in a game of Hide and Seek. Eventually the party found him talking to a giant inside a colossal box, and to reach him they had to pay to ride on the giants palm that caused Zip Zap and Baba to vomit for a minute straight. Mr. Bingles congratulated them on their win and offered an Exit for 100 tickets as well as the whereabouts of their missing escort to be on the Merry-Go-Round. The party paid, with a 40 ticket discount for good jokes, and rushed over to the attraction to find murderous clowns, turning a commoner that had been following the party into candy and eating them, blocking their exits. The party fought them to a their last breath, expended all spell slots and everyone bloodied, Baba almost dying to a balloon that dropped them from 30 feet in the air. Mr. Bingles arrived after to laugh at their suffering and promised to have more games and fun ready for the next group of rubes before the party walked away and found themselves back in the woods a few miles from Port Damali."
