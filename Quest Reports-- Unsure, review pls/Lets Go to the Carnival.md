Brief Overview: "The Party encountered a clown parade marching through Port Damali which attracted dozens of onlookers. They noticed a few peculiar figures as well as some people in the center of the parade which seemed to be towns folk, not carnival workers. As the parade dispersed and they choose to continue following the main pack, a dwarf Coinbound Guilder worker chased them down and explained not all was as it seemed. The carnival belonged to a man named Mr. Bingle and those people being shepherd along were likely being kidnapped and stopping the clowns now would be dangerous. The party had been given some tickets to attend the carnival by some of the workers and were told of a gateway into the demiplane where the carnival resides in. The party arrived at the hidden entrance and found some unconscious town guards plastered with paint but ignored them and went through the gate after learning it was infernal in nature, as well as there were almost every type of monster present besides celestials in the demiplane. The group journeyed through the carnival, trying to blend in by playing music and pretending to be long time residents, and were told the missing people (10 in total) were purchasable prizes for two tickets each. The group would go around watching the games, playing the games and talking with the kinder carnival patrons, like Bort the Bandit and Dingle the Clown. After acquiring a large sum of tickets they went to confront Mr. Bingle only to see another figure looking to purchase two of the kidnapped people for double the price, which the party didn't have to tickets to stop. Woodrow instead offered to make a deal with this figure, a deformed vampire named Bertrum, allowing the vampire to drain him a bit if they back off to which the vampire agreed and low rolled damage which could have one shot any of the other party members. Mr. Bingle allowed the party to purchase the townsfolk and the party quickly made their way out, noticing the slight decay of their surroundings as they were leaving through a generously provided doorway back to Port Damali."
