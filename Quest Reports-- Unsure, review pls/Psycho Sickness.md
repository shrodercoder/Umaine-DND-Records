Brief Overview: "The party was alerted by a town crier about a series of murders within Port Damali, three dead woman and a reward from the Coinbound for the capture of the perpetrator. The group assembled together and went to the scene, a set of apartments where the guards had begun a sloppy investigation the players had to correct. Through some difficulty the party was able to determine the killer was skilled in combat, has green blood, oversized feet that are squeeky clean, and have some relation to the circus in town. The party spoke to the other residents within the complex, a skilled hunter, the companions of the victims, a guy who saw the escape from the bathroom and came to the conclusion to first investigate a key belonging to an old unused wine cellar found under one of the victims. \n\nThe party ventured into the night and arrived at the cellar, finding a dying figure moaning about mirrors and tickets, along with a set of strange runes on the walls which begun to alight with magic, transporting the group into a circus tent within a show ring and trapped in cages. A quick skirmish with circus bears lead the party outside to find themselves inside Mr. Bingles Circus, a massive supernatural space filled with clowns, games, danger and the killer they were looking for. The party were told the only way to get back on track was to collect enough tickets to meet with Mr. Bingles, leading to about half a days worth of gaming and fighting crabs, gambling with a vampire named Bertrum, and talking to the various clowns about clues. The Party, half bloodied but recuperating with magic, journied to the Coin Dump, a Dice shaped casino building where Mr. Bingles explained the pyscho they are chasing is trying to become a new staff member for the circus. They are hiding out inside the Hall of Mirrors, but that area belongs to another patron of the Circus, The Tall Man, who resides within Funky Town. \n\nMany insight checks, talking to a very informative gnome named Simon Sesame, getting stuck in a giants Bellybutton multiple times and a ballroom dance with the Tall Man's marionettes, the group was allowed into the dungeon. A series of magical mirrors which teleported them to different rooms, requiring different solutions for each like looking into personal mirrors, walking through mazes, and escaping specters while not attacking eachother. The party eventually made it to the end of the mazes, finding a dominated guard from the complex claiming ownership of the crime though it was clear to see it was forced. Mr. Bingles, his associates, Simon Sesame and the hunter from the hotel (now transformed into a horrific clown monster) offered a deal for magic items, treasure, and components if they take the guard as the killer. After Jackie faked out the deal and smacked Mr. Bingles, the clowns, hidden dopplegangers, and the killer jumped the party in a quick, very close encounter ending up with the party restraining the hunter and the guard who might be involved in other crimes. The party was teleported back to the city and turned in the suspects."
