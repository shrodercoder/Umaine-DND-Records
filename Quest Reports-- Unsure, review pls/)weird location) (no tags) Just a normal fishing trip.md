Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** The party was tasked with dealing with a shark problem in a nearby ports town because of reports of them eating people and boats going in and out. The quest giver, an eccentric old shiphand who had knowledge on sharks was sent to collect them and help them on their task. After getting to the port, they were confronted by some sharks, which promptly got out of the water with legs and attacked the party. They managed to fight them off, but the quest giver with some REALLY bad rolls (skill issue) and being separated was promptly killed, thrown into the water, and was consumed when a giant shark came and ate him along with a boat (rip Mac). After this his ex wife (Mass) came and took the part of navigator for the rest of the quest. They decided to get a bigger boat and used one of the party members looking like the wife's child named Shark as bait for the big shark. After struggling to reel in the shark they managed to damage it enough to eventually kill it and some of its shark minions. One of the party members was still stuck inside as the shark started sinking but they managed to stop it and get them back. They ended up having to row the ship back as the shark had destroyed the rudder but it all worked out in the end! They also got Mass to break up with her toxic second husband and gave her money that she used to change her sons name to something besides Shark:)
