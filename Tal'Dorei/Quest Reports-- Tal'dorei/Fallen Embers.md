Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** Guild Members were deployed to the small village of Dultaan in the southern reaches of the Tal Dorei Republic to deal with a series of Arsons and brutal murders. Upon arriving they quickly deduced the presence of a Hellhound and its Master somewhere nearby, while attempting to buy supplies they inadvertantly encountered the foe, The shopkeeper and secret warlock of Asmodeus. After one party member slept with the villain to get a discount on an Herbal Kit, without knowing they were indeed the antagonist. After a revelation by one of the members the party chased the warlock into the corn fields where a showdown between them and the Hellhound insued. 3 party members valiantly lost thier lives, Verkong III, Johnny Pineapple, and Asynth all died in the defence of their party members. 
**Important World Impacts:** 3 Guildmembers killed, Warlock neutralized... but not her son...
