Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** - The Coinbound were summoned by Sylvenna Dawnwhisper after her cousin, Dura Xicohtli Bloodpaw of the Orroyen tribe, reported visions of destruction and dark magic emanating from the "Gauntleted City" of Hdar-Fye. Teleporting to the edge of the Rifenmist Jungle, the party navigated treacherous Iron Authority border patrols before crossing the deadly Obsidian Mazes and pressingthrough the choking Ash Plains, evading detection while approaching the necropolis-city of Hdar-Fye.
Infiltrating the sprawling Necro-Lab complex beneath the city, the party discovered that the Iron Authority had recovered the headless body of Vizier Krull—the Devastator that Ferris had beheaded in their previous encounter—and were in the process of reanimating him as a Dullahan. The party reached the Operating Theater just as the resurrection ritual completed, witnessed by the Iron Triad: Warlord Vruk "The Cinder-Lung," Artificer Xol, and High Prelate Orog. In a desperate gambit, Ferris was vortex warped onto the observation platform and attacked Xol directly, nearly dying before being rescued by his companions. The ensuing battle saw the party face the newly-risen Dullahan Devastator and its undead minions while simultaneously fending off attacks from the Triad—Orog trapped them in a blade barrier while Vruk attempted to obliterate them with a delayed blast fireball, only to have his spell counterspelled by the wizard Glee at the last possible moment. Having learned what they needed, the Triad withdrew to reassess, leaving the party to destroy both the Dullahan and the necromantic pylons powering the resurrection chamber. The party escaped with valuable intelligence, powerful magical items, and the gratitude of both Sylvenna and the Bloodpaw tribe—but also with the knowledge that they are now marked enemies of the Iron Authority at the highest levels.
**Important World Impacts** 
- Ferris Faulker had a sample of his plasmoid fluids collected, and is marked by the Iron Authority for capture. He is under daily scrying and nightly dream spells, and has a 15% chance of failing a long rest (plus psychic damage) from nightmares about the Iron Triad.
- The Iron Authority will be sending squads of Hobgoblin Devastators to burn back the Rifenmist to drive away the Orroyen tribes at its edge.
- The Iron Authority will be pivoting from necromantic experimentation to iron constructs and war machines, to better prepare for further Coinbound encounters.
- Directives from the top commanders are that All Coinbound are considered "Subjugate and capture on sight, dead or alive” by Iron Authority troops.
**Optional Observations** Still an amazing group to DM for - but a reminder that just because you’re experienced players and the highest level players in the club, you’re not the biggest fish in Exandria. Actions have consequences.
