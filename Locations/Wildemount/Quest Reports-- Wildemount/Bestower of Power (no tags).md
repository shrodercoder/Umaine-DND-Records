Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** The party was hired by a merchant Lucian to clear out an encampment of Scarlet Wing that had been attacking his caravans east of Feolinn. The party was sent to a garrison holding supplies where they met with the garrisons captain Vados who gave them a map to the encampments supposed location. Along the way they ran into a wagon which once contained goods, now completely obliterated, alongside the bodies of some novice bandits. The party followed a set of tracks leading them to a masked figure wielding an ornate scimitar, who told them about the Scarlet Wings presence in the area before leaving. The party followed his instructions and made it to the Scarlet Wing base where they fought the occupants. The party was pushed to a tough fight, and had to opt to save the life of one of their party members rather than the hostages who were all slain but they nonetheless succeeded in eliminating the Scarlet Wing. The party investigated the Scarlet Wings corpses, gaining the name of one of their officers before heading back to the garrison. While the hostages were not saved, the information gathered was enough for Vados to pay the party in full.
**Important World Impacts** The Scarlet Wing have been repelled from the outskirts of Feolinn, and information about the Scarlet Wing has been found. The masked man has also arrived, sure he won’t be relevant later.
