Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** The party was hired by a Clovis Concord agent who claimed they were having issues with a band of Kobolds stealing from its agents posted in the city of Tussoa, its Zhelezo, and even the cityfolk themselves. They were tasked with retreiving two items of importance that were stolen (Longsword, +1 and Periapt of Wound Closure) and stopping the Kobolds from stealing by any means necessary. The party entered the Kobolds' hideout (which had just been discovered by a Clovis Concord agent) with plans to negotiate, but were attacked by the front guards. The party immediately killed the attacking Kobold in self defense and went after three other Kobolds that sounded the alarm before fleeing. After killing the three fleeing Kobolds, a band of four stronger Kobolds appeared and attacked the party, allowing the other Kobolds to escape. Their leader claimed that revolution would come to those who killed their kind indiscriminately before retreating alongside the rest of its crew. 
**Important World Impacts:** A gang of Kobold thieves was chased out of their hideout on the outskirts of Tussoa. They threatened "revolution" but have since retreated and their whereabouts are currently unknown. The city of Tussoa is currently on high alert for Kobold activity.
