Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** Party was hired by representatives from Yrrosa to investigate a patrol that went missing while investigating a graveyard in the area. The party made their way to the graveyard, encountering the zombified patrol along with some undead wolves, shadows, and a blood creature. After a harrowing combat, the party emerged victorious and were able to recover the bodies of the deceased. 
**Important World Impacts:** Upon defeat, the blood creature left a note condemning the Dwendalian Empire, signed "Diligence." In addition, many of the graveyards around Yrrosa have been raided and desecrated, with their corpses missing.
