**Brief Overview:** The party was hired by the Mistborn Thieving Crew to clear their name with the city of Tussoa. The party traveled alongside one of the crewmembers to speak with the Tussoa assistant counsel (being that Marquis Tusila Latu was away on business). While discussing the terms and agreements of the crew, a group of ex-Myriad guildmembers led by an assassin attempted to kill the assistant counselors. The party managed to fend them off and the assassin and 3 scouts retreated.
**Important World Impacts** The Mistborn Thieving Crew has been cleared of their crimes in Tussoa, with the agreement that they will cease all operations and help root out the ex-Myriad syndicate from the city. A future miniature guild war between the ex-Myriad syndicate and the Mistborn Thieving Crew may break out.
