Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** he party was hired by Bargello to find the remnants of the Motley Crue that a previous Coinbound force had run into. While in Feolinn they came across a murdered noble, the killer claimed to be the Have-Not and called his doings righteous justice. After a short investigation of the scene the party pursued their quest, they quickly found the Motley Crue's leader Herminia who was being chased by the remains of the gang. After a quick confrontation the party agreed to help her in exchange for questioning them later. The party defeated the gang and questioned both Herminia and the captured gang members. Herminia told the party of the recent desertion spree that has been going on within the gangs around Feolinn and that she has no idea where the members are going. The captured gang captain revealed who bought them out, a man named the Have-Not who told them to betray Herminia. The captain then killed themselves, and the remaining lower ranked members of the gang had no information. Thus the party went back to the town and collected their rewards as well as giving the town hostage Motley Crue members to question alongside Herminia. 
A day later the party contacted Bargello for anything he was able to glean. He responded with a message, saying Herminia was slain by the Have-Not the night after they left.
**Important World Impacts** The Motley Crue has finally been pushed out of Feolinns trade routes. Also the thieves guild members are deserting their respective guilds. Where are they going? Who knows?! (Me)
**Optional Observations** Another one falls for the Motley Crue MLM scheme.
