Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** The party was hired by a merchant to protect one of his ships as it brought cargo to the Bisaft Isles. They'd been hit by a number of pirate attacks on recent voyages. On the voyage, the party discovered that the captain of their ship was collaborating with the pirates to offload the cargo and pretend it was an attack. They decided to cooperate with this scheme and got paid off for it (+100 GP per player, but they'll receive a bounty (that's a thing, right?) if someone snitches).
In Port Bisaft, I've set up 3 major factions: Clovis Concord Military (shore wardens), the Revelry, and a cult (I called them the Tideborn Communion. They're being influenced by an Aboleth, which had a temple in the area in prior eras. As of now, the Aboleth is imprisoned, but they cult wants to free it). Prior to the quest, the Revelry and local concord forces had a "live and let live" approach, since open conflict would be costly and damaging to both parties. After the party arrived, one of them decided to join the cult and was inducted, receiving a young Aboleth spawn (essentially a mind flayer tadpole) in the eye. He can now read deep speech in the region. The next day, there was a murder of a local shore warden. The party kinda solved it and handed over a pirate to the leader of the wardens to be executed. In reality, the cult orchestrated it to destabilize the region.
**Important World Impacts** The Bisaft Isles are now a site of increased conflict between the Concord and the Revelry. The cult there is proceeding with its plans.
