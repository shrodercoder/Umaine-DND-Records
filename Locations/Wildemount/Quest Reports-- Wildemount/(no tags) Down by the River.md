Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** The party was hired by the Trsotenwald town guard to clear up some Motley Crue that had washed ashore near the Ustaloch. The party quickly found the remnants of a bandit camp, which had apparently fallen under a schism after their former captain died, splitting the gang of goons into two separate sides of the lakeside. The party then found Assistant Co Captain Jerry, who was plotting to overthrow Assistant Co Captain Tom and enlisted the party’s help in doing so. The combined forces marched on, and after heavy losses from both sides, Tom was bested, leading Jerry to immediately betray the party to the surprise of absolutely nobody. The party easily bested the depleted bandit forces, killing Jerry and eliminating the bandit presence along the lake.
