**Brief Overview:** - The party was hired by Mario de Rapheli o Spaget von Spaghettio the 6th to catch the thieves who kept stealing bits and pieces of his predecessor's spaghetti that has been in a bag of colding for many years. Ulta rushed to the restaurant, quickly got a job as a cook by bragging about his cooking skills. They cooked as Mario talked to his son Michael, when the others arrived. Ulta acted as the quest-giver, and gave sub-par food for free to the party. The party was tasked with running the restaurant and guarding the spaghetti while he went to get ingredients. They served and cooked food for the customers _perfectly,_ but completely failed to notice a rat that drilled a hole in the weak wooden wall.
The thief got some pieces of pasta, and the party tracked down a splitting set of tracks - one set of boots that led to an old an abandoned house, and another to the rat. John, Tarx and Lousk followed the rat while everyone else followed the boots. Reggie used speak with animals and bribed a small bird with food to help track down whoever left the boot-tracks. While Tarx and Lousk chased down a wild-shaped rat-turned-bear unsuccessfully, Matt was left behind as a localized rainstorm pour downed in lonely melancholy. The rest of the party made their way to an abandoned house and talked to Michael, Mario's son. After a long therapy session, he agreed to talk to his dad about not wanting to carry on the family legacy. When the party returned, Michael confessed to stealing the pasta from his father. After a heated moment of screaming from his father, and some unintentional encouragement from Ulta, Michael broke his father's nose and ran away into the woods. The party searched for their missing party members, found them, and had their rewards given to them outside of the restaurant.
