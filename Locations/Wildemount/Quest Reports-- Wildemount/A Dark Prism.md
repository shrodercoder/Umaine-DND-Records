**Brief Overview:** The party went to a small village in western Dwendallian to investigate the lack of contact and a missing 7 person Dwendallian squad. Party encountered a former cleric turned necromancer attempting to create a dark prism to fuel his new found devotion to the Crawling King. The Necromancer had killed and turned over 1/6th of the 300 person town into zombies. Party rescued survivors of a local farm and relieved the surviving squad members and townsfolk in the townhall. Party then proceeded up to the chapel and confronted the Necromancer, defeating him and saving 6/7 sacrificial victims.
