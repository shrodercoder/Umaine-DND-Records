**Brief Overview:** The party was hired by a cowboy named Okko to help him find a missing horse that formerly belonged to the Hellspur gang. The party was transported to the Marquesian desert, and after some tracking they found a group of wild horses at an oasis. The party took the opportunity to tame the horses and in doing so they noticed gold dust under some of their hooves. The horses enabled them to travel speedily across the desert and gain ground on the missing horse. As they closed in on the horse a group of Hellspur riders caught up to them, prompting Okko to hold them off while the party closed in on the horse. York rode on ahead and tried to tame the Hellspur horse but was unsuccessful, causing the horse to attack. The horse put York into a very dangerous position but fortunately the rest of the party arrived in time to help. The party fought and subdued the horse after a difficult fight and turned it over to Okko, who paid them and sent them home with their mounts.
**Important World Impacts** Signs of gold have been discovered on the border between [[Hellcatch Valley]] and the [[Rumedam Desert]].
