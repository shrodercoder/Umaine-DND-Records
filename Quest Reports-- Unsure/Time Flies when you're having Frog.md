Plot Summary:
**Brief Overview:** The party was hired by Monty Curtel to investigate a series of missing people in a small lumber village. The villagers told them the missing teens had come back from a 5 day "party" with money and trinkets but went missing the same night they returned. The group split up, asking around and looking through belongings and came up with Tickets to some fair, and then discovered a group of bullywugs dressed in jester clothes hoping away from the settlement. The party chased them through the woods, slaying a few before one escaped through an archway leading to a strange populated fairground with clowns, guests, and costumed figures. The party was approached by the clown, Dingle, who told them the bullywugs were part of a "Pirate Takeover" event that lasted a bit too long and they recaptured the guest "hostages". The party were lead to the pond section of the fair to play Boat Blaster, driving boats with water cannons, to defeat the Pirate Frogs and save the Festival. Over the span of a minute, dozens of watery cannonballs were launched before the Frog King fell onto the blades of Kirym, and the kidnappees were saved. While guiding them out of the fair to return, the party partly saw the horror of this place as a dragon-like creature disguised as a horse was feasting on passed out partiers. **Important World Impacts:** Mr. Bingle's Festival of Fools is Open, tickets not included.
